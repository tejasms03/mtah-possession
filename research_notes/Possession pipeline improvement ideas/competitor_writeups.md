## 0. Compliance finding that affects our v12 (read first)

### Takeaway
Three independent teams report that an organiser clarification of 3 November (which "takes precedence" over earlier docs) forbids **any** fitting, calibration, tuning or pseudo-label/self-training on the provided match, including labels derived automatically from its Smart Tagging. Our v12 step "adaptation with weak labels from our match's Smart Tagging set pieces" appears to fall inside that ban. Smart Tagging remains allowed as an **inference input** to fixed rules or a deterministic decoder.

### Cited Findings
- verney's record of the clarification: all training, fitting, calibration and tuning (including rule thresholds, decoder costs, and "self-supervised or pseudo-label training") need external data. Labels or features derived automatically from the provided match "do not turn it into external training data". Smart Tagging, tracking and video may be inference inputs. Comparing predicted labels or overlays with the video is prohibited. Provided data must not be uploaded to AI services. — [verney ORGANIZER-CLARIFICATION.md](https://github.com/verneylmavt/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/ORGANIZER-CLARIFICATION.md)
- Derekko-S says it did no label-free adaptation on the provided game. RULES.md allowed learning without human labels, but the 3 Nov clarification requires external data for all training. The organisers' 12 Nov reply added that "anything not explicitly allowed is not permitted". — [Derekko-S TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md §8](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)
- bassem withdrew a video ball detector trained on the provided tracking projected through the homography, after organisers said labelling the provided match to train a model is not allowed "whatever the labels are derived from". It also discarded hand labels from nine windows of the match. — [bassem docs/technical_document.md §2, §5](https://github.com/bassem97/syntaxeror-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical_document.md)
- Counter-examples written before the clarification or not updated for it: minochan trains only on algorithmic pseudo-labels of the match, citing the 22–23 Oct RULES.md wording that allowed this ([minochan README](https://github.com/minochan1109/mtah2027_Y-Fi/blob/HEAD/README.md)). DSBA trains a ball-free shape model on 15,283 team and 16,759 dead pseudo-labelled frames of BAR-ATH ([DSBA SOLUTION.md](https://github.com/Feras005/mtah2027-dsba/blob/HEAD/SOLUTION.md)). hfarid built self-training on the scored game (+0.004 nested, p = 0.08) but did not ship it ([hfarid candidates/README.md](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/candidates/README.md)).
- Using match statistics that need no labels to **fit the degradation simulator** was treated as allowed by hfarid ("Only positions of the match are used, never a label") and by Wichai and tyukov. — [hfarid candidates/README.md](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/candidates/README.md); [Wichai README](https://github.com/Wichai-pan/mtah-2027-possession/blob/HEAD/README.md); [tyukov docs/technical-report.md](https://github.com/danieltyukov/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical-report.md)

### Inferences
- The safest reading is that our Smart Tagging weak-label adaptation must either be removed or replaced by a fixed inference-time prior (for example a decoder bonus with weights chosen on external data). bassem's restart prior and Derekko-S's kick-off rule both work this way. Fitting the degrader to label-free match statistics looks tolerated, but it is a grey area.
- tyukov fitted four broadcast weights and a timing shift on the public window. This is explicitly against the clarification, so treat tyukov's public-window 0.650 as contaminated.

### Gaps
- We do not have the organisers' email itself, only participants' summaries. Derekko-S and verney agree on the substance.

## 1. Team-by-team approach table

### Takeaway
The strongest documented teams (hfarid, Derekko-S, tyukov, Wichai, bassem) converge on the same pattern: train on external labelled tracking (DFL first) degraded to look like BAR-ATH, use identity-free features, predict dead/alive and team ownership, then run a 3-state Viterbi with switch costs. They differ mainly in (a) neural sequence models versus GBDT, (b) how they handle the possession-merge convention, and (c) how they fix the frozen-ball dead-ball leak.

## 2. What did the most thorough teams find most valuable, and what did they discard?

### Takeaway
Reported gains are, roughly in order: (1) learned evidence instead of hand rules (+0.26 for hfarid); (2) matching the label convention, meaning the merge threshold (hfarid's largest single gain) and the decoder switch cost (Derekko +0.014–0.028); (3) a degradation simulator calibrated to BAR-ATH's real gap structure (+0.016); (4) sequence nets plus diverse ensembling (TCN, MS-TCN, pointer; +0.01–0.03 each); (5) MBR decoding (+0.012–0.014); (6) fixing the frozen-ball leak (+0.06–0.09 on a dead-ball-moved stress view). Boundary snapping, semi-Markov/HSMM, PathCRF-style CRFs, touch/chain features, set-piece timestamps, short-spell insertion or collapse, and linear gap filling were each tried by at least one strong team and gave about 0.

### Cited Findings — what helped (with reported size)
- **Learned evidence instead of hand rules**: hand-written evidence + 3-state Viterbi scored 0.36 on DFL; GBDT on ~190 features scored 0.62. — [hfarid TECHNICAL.md §3b](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL.md). Wichai: rule tracker 0.442 → learned possession GBDT 0.525–0.559; short true possessions matched rose from 28% to 46%. — [Wichai README](https://github.com/Wichai-pan/mtah-2027-possession/blob/HEAD/README.md)
- **Output fold at the right threshold**: a 2.0 s fold on the ensemble output raised ctrl_mean from 0.696 to 0.728. After re-reading the truth as finer, raw-flag members + a 1.25 s fold gave +0.048 "fine4" over the 2 s version on all 21 game-seeds (Metrica holdout 0.678 → 0.707). — [hfarid candidates/README.md](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/candidates/README.md). "Correcting this was our largest single gain." — [hfarid TECHNICAL_SHORT.md §4](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_SHORT.md)
- **Decoder switch cost tuned to the convention**: raising the H↔A cost from 2 to 8 gave +0.014 at N = 3 (7/8 matches) and +0.028 at N = 4, but −0.003 at 1.75 s and −0.017 on raw labels. — [Derekko-P NEXT_STEPS.md](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-possession/blob/HEAD/NEXT_STEPS.md). In the final table, the D1 retune moved 0.6782 → 0.6923. — [Derekko-S TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md §4](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)
- **Realistic degradation, fitted to BAR-ATH label-free stats**: hfarid's first simulator hid the ball in 1,779 short gaps per 90 min (median 0.9 s) with 0.2 m noise. BAR-ATH has 127 gaps per 90 min (median 10.7 s), 99.7% of missing frames sit in gaps > 5 s, and ball jitter is 0.009 m. Retraining on `degrade_v2` with controlled targets: +0.016 ctrl_mean (21/21), +0.021 on event labels. — [hfarid candidates/README.md](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/candidates/README.md). Wichai found the degraded-DFL baseline score (0.231) matched the official full-match baseline (0.2433) after calibration, and used that as a realism check. — [Wichai docs/STATUS.md](https://github.com/Wichai-pan/mtah-2027-possession/blob/HEAD/docs/STATUS.md)
- **Sequence nets and diverse members** (hfarid, gains over v6 on DFL / SkillCorner): label smoothing in the TCN + wider MS-TCN +0.0099 / +0.0054; MBR instead of vote +0.0137 / +0.0123; TCN test-time averaging and adaptation + width-192 MS-TCN +0.0195 / +0.0178; adding the pointer +0.0269 / +0.0186; SkillCorner-fine-tuned MS-TCN + 19.3M pointer +0.0330 / +0.0321. The pointer was the best single model (0.764). — [hfarid TECHNICAL.md §3b, §3d](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL.md)
- **Ensembling needs diversity**: a vote over 11 early models was worse than the best single model, because 7 shared evidence and outvoted the 3 diverse ones. Votes of 5–7 models were no better than 3–4. — [hfarid TECHNICAL.md §3d, §3h](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL.md)
- **Derekko TCN ensemble**: XGBoost + TCN ensemble 0.5818 → 0.5917 (raw provider labels). Larger, longer-trained TCNs (96 channels, 5,400 steps) with cost 8 (D15): +0.041 on development and +0.021 on holdout (+0.059 on team swaps). — [Derekko-S TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md §4](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)
- **Fine-tuning on Metrica 1:1 (short fine-tune from the shipped MS-TCN)**: held-out Metrica +0.0108 (3/3), DFL −0.0026, SkillCorner −0.0015. Pooling Metrica at its natural ~4–5% share gave only +0.0020. — [hfarid TECHNICAL.md §3e](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL.md). Contrast: Derekko D30 (Metrica as training data): clean Metrica +0.019, masked views only +0.0047, DFL with the dead ball carried −0.017 to −0.059, because Metrica's corrected tracking hides the ball exactly when it is dead. — [Derekko-S TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md §4](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)
- **More external data (PFF WC22 48 games)**: hfarid v5c (DFL only) → v6 (DFL + WC): DFL 0.717 → 0.7286, WC 0.682 → 0.782. — [hfarid TECHNICAL.md §3f](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL.md). Licence conflict: Derekko-P says PFF terms §1.5(i) ban machine-learning use ([Derekko-P NEXT_STEPS.md](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-possession/blob/HEAD/NEXT_STEPS.md)), and Derekko-S used PFF for measurement only.
- **Bug fix (attack direction mirrored for one DFL game)**: +0.008. — [hfarid TECHNICAL.md §2c](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL.md)
- **Team-shape features for ball-less frames**: shape alone named the possessing side on 83% of public live frames. Adding ball-flight projection and shape trends: DFL 0.577 → 0.598. — [tyukov technical-report.md](https://github.com/danieltyukov/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical-report.md)
- **Team-swap augmentation**: tyukov's swapped set scored 0.610 vs 0.599 on DFL. — [tyukov technical-report.md](https://github.com/danieltyukov/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical-report.md)
- **bassem's external parameter fit**: mean OOS spellF1 0.470 → 0.515 (round 1) → 0.527 (round 2). Adding the classifier: 0.509 → 0.529. The restart prior from Smart Tagging was worth "about one point" on the public window. — [bassem technical_document.md §2, §5](https://github.com/bassem97/syntaxeror-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical_document.md)
- **Label-free cross-provider adaptation (external only)**: Derekko D5 self-training at 0.90 confidence on unlabelled SkillCorner: 0.493 → 0.576 on 4 confirmation matches. Not applied to the provided game. — [Derekko-S TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md §4](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)

### Cited Findings — tried and discarded (with reported effect)
- **Semi-Markov / explicit-duration decoding**: hfarid's semimarkov_lattice 0.6257 vs vote 0.6564, "within noise of the simpler decoder" ([hfarid candidates/archive/README.md](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/candidates/archive/README.md); [TECHNICAL.md §3h](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL.md)). Derekko: "Skip the HSMM rewrite", since turnover-timing headroom is only ~+0.016 after the cost retune ([Derekko-P NEXT_STEPS.md](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-possession/blob/HEAD/NEXT_STEPS.md)). verney implemented one but never validated it ([verney 03_semi_markov README](https://github.com/verneylmavt/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/implementations/03_semi_markov/README.md)).
- **PathCRF**: hfarid's linear-chain CRF with learned, frame-dependent transitions reached parity with its LGBM pipeline, with highly correlated errors (0.6113 on bench v2). ### Inferences — ideas we have NOT tried (as far as the brief says) that reportedly helped
1. **Output "control fold" with a fine threshold (~1.0–1.5 s), keeping the first post-stoppage spell** (hfarid): the largest single gain, +0.03 to +0.05 on their bench. Cheap to test on our Metrica stand-in bench, sweeping 0.75–2 s.
2. **Check our label convention against EVALUATION.md's published shift scores** (a perfect file shifted 3/8/25 frames scores 0.982/0.893/0.654). hfarid used this "length fingerprint" to infer the truth granularity without touching the GT (§3 below).
3. **Recalibrate the degrader to BAR-ATH's gap structure** (long gaps, ~80% of dead hidden, ~6% of live hidden, smooth ball, long tracklets) and retrain: +0.016 for hfarid. Wichai's "baseline-on-degraded-DFL ≈ 0.2433" test is a cheap realism check.
4. **Fix the DFL frozen-ball leak by simulating a moving dead ball** (Derekko's 20/20/60 keep/lose/handled mix, or tyukov's rolling then carried ball). +0.06–0.09 on a dead-ball-moved stress view at about −0.005 on standard views. tyukov says the leak put BAR-ATH stoppage starts 2–3.5 s late.
5. **A TCN / MS-TCN member with a boundary head, and possibly a "pointer" net over the 8 nearest players**, inside a small, diverse ensemble: +0.01 to +0.03 per member for hfarid and Derekko. Our ensemble is 5 GBDT-type members, so it may suffer from the correlated-member problem hfarid saw.
6. **Boundary-signal-discounted switch costs** (Derekko: cost cut by up to 80% when a boundary network fires). Directly targets our short-possession and boundary-timing errors.
7. **MBR decoding for expected spellF1** (+0.012–0.014 over a vote for hfarid).
8. **Test-time augmentation**: jittered input copies, offset 5 Hz grids, per-game normalisation (part of a +0.0195 step for hfarid; Derekko uses 3 offset grids).
9. **4-state decoder with "dead, home restarts" and "dead, away restarts"** plus a restart-team model (tyukov). The player over the dead ball decides the next possessor. tyukov reports no isolated ablation.
10. **Ball-flight projection features** (nearest player to the ball's predicted position 0.5/1 s ahead) and **team-shape trend features** (tyukov, part of +0.021).
11. **Short Metrica fine-tune at a 1:1 mix** (hfarid: +0.011 held-out Metrica). Note Derekko's warning that Metrica teaches the dead-ball shortcut unless its ball is degraded.

### Gaps
- No team isolated the effect of a 4-state restart decoder or of MBR on DFL-only GBDT pipelines like ours.
- hfarid's absolute numbers (0.76 on DFL) are on its own fine4 target and bench v2. They cannot be compared with Derekko's 0.709 (N = 3) or our numbers.

## 3. DFL frozen ball, SkillCorner missing ball, label conventions and the hidden truth's definition

### Takeaway
Every strong team identified the DFL freeze and SkillCorner/PFF/Metrica "ball missing = dead" as shortcut leaks, and fixed them either by **simulating a moving dead ball** (Derekko, tyukov) or by **excluding ball features from the dead model** (Wichai, DSBA). On convention, teams disagree: Derekko chose N = 3 s (minimax over 1.75–4.5 s), hfarid moved from 2 s to 1.25 s based on Metrica-event counts and the organisers' shift-score fingerprint, PML used 1.0 s, Wichai 2 s with < 3 touchers, and bassem/tyukov rely on decoder costs only.

### Cited Findings
- **DFL freeze**: "DFL freezes the ball on every dead frame". SkillCorner detects the ball on ~18% of dead vs ~91% of live frames. When the dead-frame ball instead follows the nearest player, spellF1 drops from ~0.68 to ~0.53. Fix (D2/D3): retrain with dead runs kept 20% / lost 20% / "handled" 60%, visibility 30–90%, identity swaps. The blend gives +0.062 on the dead-ball-carried view, −0.005 on standard views and +0.009 on team swaps. — [Derekko-S TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md §5](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)
- tyukov: models learned "a ball that does not move is dead" and put BAR-ATH stoppage starts 2–3.5 s late. Fix: a rolling, decelerating dead ball carried to the restart spot, with jittered gap edges. — [tyukov technical-report.md](https://github.com/danieltyukov/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical-report.md)
- Wichai: clean ball-only features reach dead F1 0.996 on DFL (the leak); player-only features reach 0.89–0.90 (the realistic number). The player-only model was kept. — [Wichai README](https://github.com/Wichai-pan/mtah-2027-possession/blob/HEAD/README.md)
- bassem: PFF has the ball on 1% of dead frames, against "two thirds" in the public window, so a PFF-trained classifier learns "ball seen = alive" (dead recall 0.55 vs 0.72). Drawing a synthetic dead-ball path repaired the statistics but not the ranking. The source that transfers best is the one whose ball and camera behave like BAR-ATH's during stoppages. — [bassem technical_document.md §2](https://github.com/bassem97/syntaxeror-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical_document.md)
- **Conflicting reports on BAR-ATH ball visibility during stoppages**: bassem says the ball is seen on about two thirds of dead frames in the public window. DSBA says ~65% of BAR-ATH dead frames are tracked. tyukov says 77% of missing frames fall inside predicted stoppages (and 19% outside). hfarid's mixture fit puts ~95% of long-gap time at dead ball, with its simulator hiding ~80% of dead frames. These describe different quantities (share of dead frames with a ball vs share of missing frames that are dead), so they are not strictly contradictory, but they imply different simulator settings. — sources as cited; [DSBA SOLUTION.md](https://github.com/Feras005/mtah2027-dsba/blob/HEAD/SOLUTION.md)
- **Derekko N = 3 rule**: external anchors are Metrica raw events ~1.75 s, Metrica with a control rule 4.25–4.5 s, and SkillCorner phases 4.0 s. N = 3 minimises the worst-case loss. It is used only to score candidates, not applied to output. If the truth counts every touch, the settings cost ~0.01–0.02. — [Derekko-S TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md §6, §8](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md); [Derekko-P NEXT_STEPS.md](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-possession/blob/HEAD/NEXT_STEPS.md)
- **hfarid's convention evidence**: DFL folds give 412 / 344 / 272 / 224 possessions per 90 at T = 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 s. Metrica's own event rules give 305–331 per 90 with 17–22% of spells < 2 s, implying T ≈ 1.2–1.5 s. The organisers' shift scores (0.982 / 0.893 / 0.654 at 3 / 8 / 25 frames late) put the truth's length fingerprint "between the raw DFL flag and a 1 s fold". A 2 s target gives 0.999 / 0.989 / 0.797. Final fold: 1.25 s. If the truth were 2 s, the fine output would cost ~0.04. — [hfarid candidates/README.md](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/candidates/README.md); [hfarid TECHNICAL_SHORT.md](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_SHORT.md)
- **Public-window facts quoted by teams**: 17 possessions, mean 14.2 s, median 7.6 s, 1 of 17 under 1 s (0.8 s and 1.3 s ones exist) ([Wichai README](https://github.com/Wichai-pan/mtah-2027-possession/blob/HEAD/README.md)). The truth "changes the owning team at a touch"; there are possessions of 19 and 33 frames; switches sit at a kick or reception by the new team ([bassem §1](https://github.com/bassem97/syntaxeror-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical_document.md)). The public window has 4.2 possession changes per live minute vs 7.8–9 in DFL ([tyukov](https://github.com/danieltyukov/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical-report.md)). The public window is "the fastest five minutes of the match" ([bassem](https://github.com/bassem97/syntaxeror-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical_document.md)).
- Wichai rule R9: the truth is Metrica's corrected tracking, mechanically mapped (D where it marks the ball dead, otherwise its owner). — [Wichai docs/possession_rules.md](https://github.com/Wichai-pan/mtah-2027-possession/blob/HEAD/docs/possession_rules.md)
- **Kick-off rule**: the 2,934 graded frames 110,808–113,741 before the second-half kick-off are D (organisers' FAQ). Implemented by Derekko-S (via the Smart Tagging "Kick Off (Start)" tag), hfarid, bassem, PML, Matra, tyukov. Wichai found its v2 labelled 1,013 of those frames as H before the fix. — [Wichai docs/STATUS.md §5](https://github.com/Wichai-pan/mtah-2027-possession/blob/HEAD/docs/STATUS.md); [Derekko-S §6](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)
- **X abstention is useless**: the scorer forward-fills X, so it has zero spellF1 value and hurts macroF1. — [Derekko-S §6](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md); [Derekko-P NEXT_STEPS.md](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-possession/blob/HEAD/NEXT_STEPS.md)
- **Full-match prediction statistics reported** (useful as a sanity range): hfarid 262–277 possessions, dead share 0.48. bassem 277 possessions, 43% dead. tyukov 293 possessions, 50% dead, 120 stoppages. Wichai 48% dead. Albert 434 spells (likely over-split). — sources as above

### Inferences
- If our bench target uses a ~3 s merge (Derekko-like), hfarid's evidence suggests the truth may be finer (~1–1.5 s). Our "short possessions" error may partly be a convention mismatch. Scoring our bench at several merge thresholds (raw, 0.5, 1, 1.25, 2, 3 s), as hfarid's "fine4" and Derekko's sensitivity table do, would show how exposed we are.
- The organisers' shift-score fingerprint is a legitimate, GT-free way to pick between conventions. Compute the same shift curve on our Metrica stand-in targets at each merge threshold and match it.

### Gaps
- No team has access to the true merge threshold. The 1.25 s vs 3 s disagreement between the two most thorough teams is unresolved.

## 4. Audio/whistles, video ball detection, Smart Tagging, neural sequence models, PathCRF, BallRadar

### Takeaway
No team used audio. Video ball or touch detection was tried and dropped (bassem: rule-banned because it was trained on the match; hfarid: no gain; Derekko: dropped). ## 5. Where spellF1 is lost

### Takeaway
Two independent oracle analyses agree that **ownership (which team), and especially short possessions (< 2–5 s), dominates the loss**. Perfect stoppages add only +0.014 to +0.03. Perfect boundaries add +0.035 to +0.07. All pipelines **under-predict the number of possessions** (Derekko 6–25% fewer, tyukov 71% of truth, hfarid 2,245 vs 3,061), with high precision and low recall.

### Cited Findings
- **Derekko D11/D21 (oracle substitution)**: with true ownership, combined spellF1 rises 0.723 → 0.885. With true stoppages only to 0.750 (D3: 0.682 → 0.887 / 0.714). D3 predicted 20–25% fewer spells than the targets and recovered under half of true spells < 5 s. D15 predicts 6–22% fewer and recovers 46–52% of SkillCorner spells of 1.75–5 s. Boundary offsets have medians of −2 to +2 frames. On clean DFL the submission predicts 263 possessions vs 281 true and matches 93 of 184 ownership changes within 1 s. — [Derekko-S TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md §4, §8](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md); [Derekko-S README.md](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/README.md). Earlier oracle: "Better ownership probabilities remain the largest oracle (+0.23 DFL / +0.21 SkillCorner)". — [Derekko-P NEXT_STEPS.md](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-possession/blob/HEAD/NEXT_STEPS.md)
- **hfarid error budget** (21 game-seeds, 9,183 true spells): recall is 0.03 for spells < 1 s (12.5% of spells), 0.38 for 1–3 s (30.5%), 0.70 for 3–8 s and 0.91 above 8 s. Miss reasons: wrong team 41%, predicted dead 21%, merged into a same-team spell 22%, boundary off 16%. In 64% of missed spells the ball was visible throughout. Oracle headroom: true boundaries +0.072; true labels inside our boundaries +0.029; perfect dead ball +0.014; true sub-1 s spells pasted in +0.085; sub-2 s +0.169. — [hfarid candidates/README.md](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/candidates/README.md)
- **Wichai**: 49% of true spells have best IoU < 0.3 (median ~4 s), 28% have 0.3–0.7, 24% ≥ 0.7. Oracle boundary snapping within ±3 s: D +0.018, H↔A +0.018, both +0.035. Missed spells had the ball visible on 38% of frames vs 57% for matched ones, and a median of 0.04 s of clear touches by the possessing team. "It pays to skip [short possessions] rather than guess." — [Wichai docs/STATUS.md](https://github.com/Wichai-pan/mtah-2027-possession/blob/HEAD/docs/STATUS.md)
- **bassem**: open-play team transitions land 1–3 s off in both directions. Proximity fires before the touch; touch detection misses soft receptions and misattributes ~1 in 5; headers happen metres from the projected ball. Fouls enter D seconds late because the ball keeps rolling at 8–12 m/s. ### Inferences
- Our stated main errors (short possessions on the team side, and boundary timing) match the other teams' findings. The strongest documented levers for short possessions are: (a) fold threshold and convention (hfarid), (b) boundary-aware sequence models and boundary-discounted switch costs (Derekko, hfarid MS-TCN), and (c) longer and larger TCN training (Derekko D15 raised clean-DFL predicted possessions from 219 to 263 vs 281 true). Short-spell insertion heuristics, touch features and boundary snapping all failed for at least one strong team.
- The "predicted dead" miss category (21% for hfarid) together with flicker on out-of-pitch balls suggests checking our dead/live flicker when the tracked ball is outside the lines on BAR-ATH.
- Expect our public-window 0.59 to say little. hfarid's versions swung 0.567–0.708 on the window while their bench ranking was monotonic.
