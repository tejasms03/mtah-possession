# Ship compliant v6, then fix kick-off frames

**Rule risk first: the most valuable change before Saturday's freeze is not an accuracy gain. It is dropping the training on our own match that v7–v12 depend on.** Three other teams report an organiser clarification that bans *all* fitting, calibration, tuning and pseudo-label or self-supervised training on the provided match, including labels derived automatically from its Smart Tagging. The verneylmavt write-up records it, and Derekko-web and bassem97 cite it. Smart Tagging and tracking may still be used as inference inputs to fixed calculations ([verney ORGANIZER-CLARIFICATION.md](https://github.com/verneylmavt/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/ORGANIZER-CLARIFICATION.md); [Derekko-S §8](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md); [bassem §2, §5](https://github.com/bassem97/syntaxeror-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical_document.md)). This contradicts the public RULES.md, which still lists "pseudo-labels produced by an algorithm" as fine on the provided game ([RULES.md](../vendor/mtah/RULES.md)). v12's `model.own` step retrains both dead-ball models on weak labels from our match's 60 Smart Tagging set pieces, so it falls inside the ban. The compliant alternative costs nothing measurable. **v6 + idea-2 features scored 0.7724 on the whole-match Metrica stand-in bench, against 0.7721 for v12** ([DECISIONS.md](../docs/DECISIONS.md)).

The second finding is a free correctness fix. The organisers' FAQ says frames **110,808–113,741 are graded and all `D`**, because the second half kicks off at 113,742 ([FAQ.md](../vendor/mtah/FAQ.md)). Our current v12 file labels **2,034 of those 2,934 frames H or A, in six spurious H/A runs**, and v6 does the same (our measurement of `outputs/models_v12.csv`). Beyond those two fixes, the only well-evidenced lever for our error profile (short possessions, under-splitting) is retuning the team-switch decoder on external labels at a finer possession granularity. A learned switch-hazard model is a time-boxed second bet. Most other ideas either gave about zero for stronger teams, or need training or calibration on our match and are now excluded. The organisers themselves say **spellF1 differences below about 0.04 are noise on the full match** ([EVALUATION.md](../vendor/mtah/EVALUATION.md)). So with 1.5 days left, ideas should be ranked by risk first and gain second.

## The early-November clarification turns v7–v12 into a liability

Derekko-web and verneylmavt date the clarification **3 November**; our own notes had about 4 November. Derekko-S adds that the organisers' **12 November reply said "anything not explicitly allowed is not permitted"** ([Derekko-S §8](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)). verney's record lists rule thresholds, decoder costs and "self-supervised or pseudo-label training" as things that need external data. It also says labels derived automatically from the provided match "do not turn it into external training data" ([verney](https://github.com/verneylmavt/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/ORGANIZER-CLARIFICATION.md)). bassem withdrew a video ball detector because the organisers said labelling the provided match to train a model is not allowed "whatever the labels are derived from" ([bassem](https://github.com/bassem97/syntaxeror-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical_document.md)). We have only participants' summaries, not the email itself. Three independent teams agree on the substance, though, and a written ruling outranks a README. **Our DECISIONS.md entry 49 kept v11b over a tied v6 on the grounds that adaptation "costs nothing". That reasoning no longer holds, because it now carries disqualification risk for zero measured gain.**

The table below sorts every adaptation-type idea in the research by this rule. "Compliant" means the pipeline uses only external data plus fixed calculations at inference time. "Grey" means reasonable teams disagree, or the activity sits close to the wording.

| Idea | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `model.own` weak dead/alive labels from Smart Tagging (v7, v8, v9, v11b, v12) | **Non-compliant** | Pseudo-label training on the provided match |
| `model.own_team` touch-run labels (v10), one-round self-training on decoded segments | **Non-compliant** | Same |
| Per-match class-prior shift to a dead-ball share implied by our Smart Tagging | **Non-compliant** | Calibrates outputs on the provided match |
| Importance weighting of source frames by similarity to our match (adversarial classifier) | **Non-compliant** | Fits weights using target frames |
| Whistle detector with thresholds self-calibrated on our kick-off tags | **Non-compliant** | Calibration on the provided match |
| Adversarial validation used to *choose* which features to drop | **Grey, avoid** | Unlabelled target frames steer model selection. Acceptable only as a diagnostic that changes nothing |

Two pieces of hygiene follow for the technical document. First, DECISIONS.md admits that **v1–v6 keep/reject decisions were originally made on the public window** before `possession select` replaced them. A few constants still carry public-window justifications in their comments: `dead_ball.speed_ref_mps`, and `min_dead_frames` ("99% of dead spells in DFL, Metrica and the public GT"). Re-justify each from DFL/Metrica alone, which the 99% statistic already supports. Second, record where `w_tag_dead`, `w_tag_team` and the stand-in `tag_lead_s` of 3 s came from. If any was read off our match's tag-to-restart lag, the technical document should say so and call it a fixed constant, not a fit. A small fix while editing: the `min_possession_frames: 10` comment says 0.6 s, but 10 frames at 25 fps is 0.4 s.

## A free fix: 2,934 graded frames before kick-off are all `D`

The FAQ calls this window "one trap worth knowing" ([FAQ.md](../vendor/mtah/FAQ.md)). Wichai found its own v2 labelling 1,013 of these frames as H ([Wichai STATUS.md §5](https://github.com/Wichai-pan/mtah-2027-possession/blob/HEAD/docs/STATUS.md)). Derekko-S (via the Smart Tagging "Kick Off (Start)" tag), hfarid, bassem, PML, Matra and tyukov all force it to D ([Derekko-S §6](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)). Our decoder treats the whole of `HALVES[1]` (110,808–190,197) as playable, and v12 outputs H at 110,808, A at 111,591, H at 111,676, A at 112,043, D at 112,135, H at 112,607, D at 113,290 and H again at 113,718. Every one of those H/A runs is a false positive against an all-D truth. The last one also starts the real first possession 24 frames early.

The size of the gain is our arithmetic, not a measurement. Removing about five false positives from about 255 predicted possessions raises precision by about 2%. That lifts spellF1 by **roughly +0.005 to +0.01**, slightly more than most decoder tweaks deliver, for **about 30 minutes of work and no tuning**. The Metrica bench cannot measure it because it has no such window, so it is a correctness fix, not a bench-selected change. Implement it from the Smart Tagging kick-off tag or `games.half_video_blocks("BAR-ATH")` rather than a hard-coded frame number. That keeps it a fixed calculation on an inference input. Afterwards, confirm on the public-window output only that the file is still well-formed.

## Short possessions are a granularity problem the decoder can partly fix

Our error profile is the same as every strong team's. Pipelines under-split, and short spells drive the loss. hfarid's error budget over 9,183 true spells shows **recall of 0.03 for spells under 1 s and 0.38 for 1–3 s**, against 0.91 above 8 s. Pasting in true sub-2 s spells would add +0.169, against only +0.014 for perfect dead ball ([hfarid candidates/README.md](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/candidates/README.md)). Derekko's oracle agrees: true ownership lifts spellF1 from 0.723 to 0.885, while true stoppages reach only 0.750 ([Derekko-S §4](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)). Our v12 file shows the symptom. It has **255 possessions over about 106 graded minutes (about 2.4 per minute), only 15 of them under 2 s**, and **51% D**. Our Metrica stand-in labels run at 3.4–3.9 possessions per minute ([DECISIONS.md](../docs/DECISIONS.md)), and other teams' full-match outputs have 262–293 possessions with 43–50% D ([hfarid](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/candidates/README.md); [tyukov](https://github.com/danieltyukov/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical-report.md)). Note that the v7 adaptation pushed our D share from 47% to 51%, so dropping it moves the D share back toward the field.

The lever with the best evidence is the possession *granularity* the decoder is tuned to. hfarid called moving its output fold from 2 s to 1.25 s "our largest single gain", worth **+0.03 to +0.05** on its bench ([hfarid TECHNICAL_SHORT.md §4](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_SHORT.md)). Derekko shows that the sign depends on the convention. Raising the H↔A switch cost from 2 to 8 gave **+0.014 against 3 s-merged targets but −0.017 against raw labels** ([Derekko-P NEXT_STEPS.md](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-possession/blob/HEAD/NEXT_STEPS.md)). Our team dials were tuned on all 10 matches, and the IDSSE part of that set uses `merge_blip_s: 2.0`. That is the coarse convention hfarid moved away from. The compliant experiment is to cache model probabilities for each stand-in, then sweep `lambda_ha`, `lambda_ha_touch`, `temperature`, `w_team` and `min_possession_frames`. Score against the Metrica event labels, which come from the same provider as the organisers' truth, and optionally against DFL re-merged at 1.0–1.25 s. Decoding is cheap compared with retraining. Adopt a setting only from the centre of a flat plateau, only if it clears the pre-set **+0.01 mean** bar, and only if no stand-in gets worse. Three matches is a small sample, and our bench noise is about 0.01.

The second, riskier bet is a **learned switch-hazard model**. This is a second gradient-boosted model, trained on DFL and Metrica, that predicts "team possession changes within ±k frames". Its −log p would cut the Viterbi H↔A cost, in place of or on top of the constant touch-gated cost. It is the GBDT version of three published results. PathCRF's context-dependent transitions added about **+1.9 event-F1 points** over static ones, and masking impossible transitions still mattered ([PathCRF](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12080)). ASRF's boundary branch added **+8.8 to +10.6 segment-F1** in action segmentation ([ASRF](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2007.06866)). Derekko ships switch costs cut by **up to 80% when its boundary head fires** ([Derekko-S](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)). The counter-evidence comes from GBDT teams. verney's learned H↔A adjuster scored **+0.001 inner and −0.002 on confirmation** ([verney IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md](https://github.com/verneylmavt/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/implementations/04_classical_ml/IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md)). tyukov's second-stage stacker gained **+0.009 on DFL but was dropped because it broke under domain shift** ([tyukov](https://github.com/danieltyukov/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical-report.md)). It costs 4–6 hours with a retrain and a bench run, so time-box it to Friday.

Feature work ranks below both, and idea 2 already covers much of it. tyukov's ball-flight projection (nearest player to the ball's position 0.5 s and 1 s ahead) plus shape-trend features moved its DFL score **0.577 → 0.598** ([tyukov](https://github.com/danieltyukov/more-than-a-hack-2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical-report.md)). hfarid found pitch-control features "inside noise" and no help with under-splitting ([hfarid archive](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/candidates/archive/README.md)). Our idea 2 already has ball-heading-to-team, carrier pressure and touch runs. An interception-race feature (Spearman time-to-intercept along the extrapolated ball path, using the Friends of Tracking defaults: 5 m/s, 0.7 s reaction, σ 0.45 s; [LaurieOnTracking](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Friends-of-Tracking-Data-FoTD/LaurieOnTracking/master/Metrica_PitchControl.py)) is the one genuinely new signal. It is worth trying only if a second person is free.

## Boundary snapping, dead-ball templates and whistles do not pay before Saturday

Several ideas look promising in the literature but failed repeatedly in practice. **Boundary snapping or shifting** gave +0.001 at 1 s and worse at 2–3 s on our own bench, +0.0004 for Wichai, and +0.0003 for Derekko's 2-frame shift ([DECISIONS.md](../docs/DECISIONS.md); [Wichai](https://github.com/Wichai-pan/mtah-2027-possession/blob/HEAD/docs/STATUS.md); [Derekko-S](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)). Our measured 0.5–2 s lead of truth switches over our first touch is therefore not fixable by post-processing. **Semi-Markov duration models** landed "within noise" for hfarid, and Derekko skipped them because turnover-timing headroom after a cost retune was only about +0.016. **Touch and possession-chain features** scored −0.0004 for Derekko. **Short-spell insertion** and a per-spell keep/hand-back classifier gave 0 to +0.002 for hfarid. **Seed or slice ensembling** gave nothing: our v11b vs v7 was 0.7576 vs 0.7573, and Derekko's 3-seed average scored 0.717 vs 0.723. So the compliant version should drop the 5-member ensemble too, unless a single bench run of "v6 + idea 2 + ensemble 5" clears +0.01. That also cuts the roughly 35–40 minute single-thread build.

Dead-ball work has a low ceiling. Perfect stoppages are worth only **+0.014 (hfarid) to +0.03 (Derekko)** in oracle tests. Simulated restart timestamps as decoder evidence scored 0.61376 vs 0.61382 for Derekko. bassem's three attempts to fix late foul stoppages all failed ([bassem §2](https://github.com/bassem97/syntaxeror-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/docs/technical_document.md)). Vidal-Codina's restart templates are the most portable rules. They place a thrower beyond the touchline minus ε, a player near a corner mark, or a keeper in the goal area ([Vidal-Codina et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00804v3)). But their tolerances live in an unretrieved supplement, and even Lang's best player-only model reaches only **74% recall on throw-ins** with ±1.7 s edge error ([Lang et al. 2022](https://rcastoragev2.blob.core.windows.net/da8383a4790e53f7460e9c4a819d6937/PMC9522646.pdf)). Simulating a moving dead ball in DFL (Derekko's 20/20/60 keep/lose/handled mix) gave **+0.062 on a stress view but −0.005 on standard views** ([Derekko-S §5](https://github.com/Derekko-web/mtah2027-submission/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL_DOCUMENT.md)). Our model A already sidesteps the frozen-ball leak by dropping ball-motion features and averaging in a Metrica dead model.

**Whistle audio is out.** No team used it. Under IFAB guidance the whistle is not needed for throw-ins, corners or goal kicks ([IFAB guideline](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/guidelines/body-language-communication-and-whistle/)), which are **48 of our 59 tagged restarts**. The best published detector reaches 93% recall and 88% precision, but its thresholds are not published ([Raventós et al. 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4485672)). Calibrating them on our kick-off tags is now non-compliant. A fixed-threshold version would be legal, but none of the Metrica stand-ins has audio to validate it on. I could not locate the match video on this machine, so whether it has an audio track at all is still unchecked.

| 4 | Switch-hazard GBDT discounting H↔A cost | Compliant | 0 to +0.02 (PathCRF +1.9 pts; verney ±0.002) | 4–6 h | Medium | **Time-box Friday; bench Saturday morning** |

The sequence follows from the table. By Friday evening, #1 and #2 become the new safe submission, reproduced byte-identically from a fresh clone. #3 runs on cached probabilities in parallel with #4. On Saturday morning, `possession select` decides whether #3 and #4 clear the pre-set bar against that safe baseline. Freeze happens Saturday afternoon whatever the outcome. The public window is used throughout only to check that the frozen file is well-formed and aligned, never to choose between candidates ([RULES.md](../vendor/mtah/RULES.md); [EVALUATION.md](../vendor/mtah/EVALUATION.md)). hfarid's own versions swung between 0.567 and 0.708 there while ranking monotonically on large benchmarks ([hfarid TECHNICAL.md §4b](https://github.com/hfarid19/camp-nou-limits-mtah2027/blob/HEAD/TECHNICAL.md)).

## Conclusion

Our pipeline is already at the point where the field's best teams stalled. GBDT evidence plus a constrained Viterbi decoder captures most of what is available without neural sequence models, and every remaining structural idea shows gains no bigger than the noise of a single full match. The decisive variables for this submission are therefore not modelling choices. They are rule compliance, the second-half kick-off window, and whether our decoder matches the truth's possession granularity. The first two are certain wins at almost no cost. The third is the only place where a few hours could plausibly move the score by a few hundredths.

One uncertainty matters more than any idea above: nobody outside the organisers knows the truth's merge convention. hfarid's evidence points to about 1.25 s and Derekko's minimax to 3 s, and a wrong guess costs about 0.01–0.04 either way. Our Metrica stand-ins come from the same provider as the truth, which makes them the best compliant proxy available. A retune should therefore trust those three matches over DFL. If the bench stays flat across granularities, keep the current dials. Being moderately wrong in a robust way beats being precisely tuned to the wrong convention.
